ZHURT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

ZHURT ("we", "the app") connects the Kazakh community in Istanbul: a map of friends, help from people nearby, a city-wide help board, help chats and news from the Kazakh council. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account. Your email address and password (stored securely by our login provider), or your Google or Apple account if you sign in with one.

Profile. Nickname, full name, gender, university and study year, and your avatar or profile photo. Others see your nickname, university, avatar and photo. Your full name is never shown to other users.

Optional details. Phone number, student number and Kimlik number, if you choose to add them. Only you can see them.

Location. While the app is open and you are not in Ghost Mode, your location is shared with your friends on the map. New accounts start in Ghost Mode, so nobody sees you until you turn sharing on. When you post a nearby help request, its location is shown to people inside the radius you chose. We do not track you in the background.

Help requests and chats. What you post, offers you make, chat messages, and the ratings you give and receive.

Device. A notification token so we can send you alerts.

AI guide. Questions you type to the AI guide are sent to Google's Gemini service to produce an answer.

How we use it

To run the app: show friends on the map, deliver help requests to people nearby, run chats, send notifications, show council news, and keep the community safe (blocking, reports, ratings). We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who processes it

  • Supabase – database, login, file storage.
  • Google Firebase – push notifications.
  • Mapbox – map display.
  • Google – Google sign-in and the Gemini AI guide.
  • Apple – Sign in with Apple and notifications on iPhone.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Safety and reports

Reports you send go to the Kazakh council moderators in Istanbul, together with the reported content, so they can act on them.

How long we keep it

Nearby help requests expire within hours. Your account data stays until you delete your account. When you delete it in Settings → Delete account, your profile, photos, requests, chats and friendships are removed. Requests where you helped someone stay in their history without your name. Database backups that may still contain your data are overwritten within 30 days.

Your choices

You can turn on Ghost Mode at any time, edit or remove optional details and your photo, block or report people, turn off notifications, and delete your account from inside the app.

Children

ZHURT is for people aged 16 and over.
